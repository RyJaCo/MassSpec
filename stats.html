Load a table to start

The analyser expects tidy data: one row per measurement, one column per variable. For example:

Sample,Mouse,Treatment,Genotype,Intensity
S001,M01,Vehicle,WT,3359143
S002,M01,Vehicle,WT,3274759
S003,M02,High dose,KO,5189015
  • Pick the value column(s), the group column and, if the same subject appears in several groups, the subject column (that makes it a paired design).
  • Rows that share a subject and group are treated as technical replicates and averaged, so n counts biological replicates.
  • A table with one column per condition (wide format) works too: choose “Compare columns”.

Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Runs entirely in your browser. Test p-values are checked against SciPy and statsmodels; the advice is a guide, not a substitute for thinking about your design. The example tables are simulated.
